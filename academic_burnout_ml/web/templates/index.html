    Institutional Clinical Triage & Intervention Framework

    Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) for University Counselors, Faculty Advisors, and Student Affairs Officers.

    Tier 1: Low Risk (Score < 10)

    Student exhibits healthy coping mechanisms, adequate sleep architecture, and sustainable study habits.

    • Routine wellness monitoring through periodic self-checkins
    • Encourage active participation in recreational campus sports
    • Provide optional stress resilience and time-management workshops

    Tier 2: Medium Risk (Score 10 – 20)

    The Golden Intervention Window: Sub-clinical stress accumulation with sleep reduction and digital screen overload.

    • Automatic notification to departmental Academic Mentor
    • Academic deadline rebalancing to prevent clustered submissions
    • Enrollment in guided digital detox and circadian sleep hygiene clinics
    • Peer mentorship pairing to buffer against competitive pressure

    Tier 3: High Risk (Score > 20)

    Acute emotional exhaustion, depersonalization, and elevated anxiety indicating imminent burnout collapse.

    • Immediate triage session with Licensed Campus Clinical Psychologist
    • Coordination with Academic Dean for temporary course-load reduction
    • Confidential family notification and support network engagement
    • Mandatory bi-weekly counseling and clinical progress tracking
